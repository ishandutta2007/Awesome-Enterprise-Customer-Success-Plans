## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Salesforce Success Plans](https://www.salesforce.com/services/success-plans/)**  

  Three-tier support program: **Standard** (included with all licenses) provides self-guided resources, knowledge articles, documentation, and Trailhead access; **Premier** (30% of net license fees) adds expert guidance, product education, health checks, and 24/7 support for business-blocking issues; **Signature** (contact account executive) includes a designated Customer Success Manager, Customer Success Score, proactive monitoring, key event management, and fastest 24/7 response times .



- **[AWS Enterprise Support](https://aws.amazon.com/premiumsupport/plans/enterprise/)**  

  Enterprise-grade support with designated Technical Account Manager (TAM), 15-minute response for production-critical cases, AWS Security Incident Response at no additional cost, strategic business reviews, and AWS Countdown event management. Minimum $5,000/month (reduced from $15,000). Enterprise On-Ramp customers automatically upgraded to Enterprise Support throughout 2026 .



- **[Microsoft Unified Enterprise Support](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/docs/)**  

  Comprehensive support package with proactive services, success management, and 24/7 reactive problem resolution. Base package includes core support; Flex Add-on allows purchasing additional proactive services, extended solutions, or custom services as needed. Available with reactive problem resolution prioritized 24/7 .



- **[Google Cloud Premium Support](https://cloud.google.com/support/docs/premium)**  

  Enterprise support with Customer Technical Advisor (TAM) services, Operational Health Reviews, Event Management Service for peak events, and training credits (6,250 per year for Google Cloud Skills Boost). P1 case response within 15 minutes. Value-Add Services include Mission Critical Services with 5-minute P0 response and Assured Support for compliance requirements .



- **[ServiceNow Impact](https://www.servicenow.com/success.html)**  

  Digital experience platform for consuming and managing Impact resources. Includes product implementation playbooks, best practices content library, adoption toolkits for each product, and on-demand training courses. Provides visualization of helpful data and communication with Impact Team .



- **[Adobe Platinum Support](https://helpx.adobe.com/support/programs/enterprise-support-programs1.html)**  

  Comprehensive package for mission-critical Adobe applications. Includes unlimited incidents, 24x7x365 support for Priority 1 issues, 30-minute target response time, 10 authorized contacts, bundled maintenance, and optional Technical Account Manager. Available as annual contract for predictable costs .



- **[Oracle Premier Support](https://www.oracle.com/support/)**  

  Unified support for the complete Oracle stack with single point of accountability. Includes 24/7 access to My Oracle Support, proactive tools for problem prevention, health checks, patch recommendations, and access to 16,000+ support professionals globally. Oracle Platinum Services available at no additional cost for engineered systems .



- **[Cisco Success Tracks](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en_us/customer-experience/pdfs/cisco-success-tracks-customer-faq.pdf)**  

  Trusted Support with 24-hour daily online and phone access, 30-minute critical response, Solution Support for multi-vendor issues, hardware replacement options, and Expert Resources including Ask the Experts sessions, Accelerators for one-on-one coaching, and Specialized Expertise fee-based options .



- **[Workday Enterprise Management Services](https://www.workday.com/)**  

  Support services for Workday's Enterprise Management Cloud covering HR, Finance, and Planning. Provides deployment guidance, adoption resources, and ongoing optimization support for enterprise customers.



- **[SAP Premium Engagement](https://help.sap.com/docs/SUPPORT_CONTENT/sm/3518048023.html)**  

  Tiered engagement model including SAP ActiveAttention, SAP MaxAttention, and SAP ActiveEmbedded. Provides pre-packaged services for Business Process Automation Checks and SAP S/4HANA Preparation including Data Quality Pre-Checks .



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Znuny](https://github.com/znuny/znuny)**  

  Fully customizable open-source help desk platform (fork of OTRS). Features customer interface for self-service, FAQ/knowledge base, ticket creation via multiple channels, SLA management, and 3rd-party system integration. 10+ years of development, 30K+ GitHub commits, 200K+ forum posts. On-premise or cloud deployment .



- **[FreeScout](https://github.com/freescout-help-desk/freescout)**  

  Super lightweight, self-hosted Help Scout and Zendesk alternative (AGPL-3.0) built with PHP/Laravel. No limitations on users, tickets, or mailboxes. 100% mobile-friendly, multilingual (23+ languages), seamless email integration, collision detection, push notifications, and internal notes.



- **[OpenSupports](https://github.com/opensupports/opensupports)**  

  Simple and beautiful open-source ticket system (GPL-3.0) with customer portal and knowledge base. Lightweight and easy to deploy. Features ticket management, agent assignment, and self-service capabilities.



- **[Frappe Helpdesk](https://github.com/frappe/helpdesk)**  

  Modern, streamlined, 100% open-source ticket management tool built on Frappe Framework with Vue-based UI. Features dual portals for agents and customers, customizable SLAs, assignment rules, knowledge base with advanced search, saved replies, and production-ready Docker deployment.



- **[Zammad](https://github.com/zammad/zammad)**  

  Open-source helpdesk and customer support platform (AGPL-3.0). Features ticketing, live chat, telephony integration, knowledge base, and multi-channel support. Web-based customer portal with ticket tracking and self-service capabilities.



- **[Chatwoot](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot)**  

  Open-source customer engagement platform (MIT) with omnichannel inbox supporting live chat, email, SMS, and social channels. Features agent assignment, canned responses, automation, and embeddable widgets.



- **[UVdesk](https://github.com/uvdesk/community-skeleton)**  

  Open-source helpdesk system (MIT) built on Symfony. Features customer portal, knowledge base, ticket management, and multi-channel support with e-commerce integrations.



- **[Helpy](https://github.com/helpyio/helpy)**  

  Open-source helpdesk and customer support platform (MIT) built on Rails. Features multi-channel support, knowledge base, community forums, and ticket management.



- **[osTicket](https://github.com/osTicket/osTicket)**  

  Widely-used open-source ticketing system (GPL-2.0). Features customer portal, ticket submission, knowledge base, and email piping. Mature and stable with extensive plugin ecosystem.



- **[Tiledesk](https://github.com/Tiledesk/tiledesk)**  

  Open-source conversational platform (MIT) with live chat and chatbot. Features customer support portal, knowledge base integration, and multi-channel engagement.



- **[Second Sight](https://github.com/topics/customer-success)**  

  Data-driven customer success tool listed on GitHub's customer-success topic. Helps CS teams track account health, adoption metrics, and renewal risk signals .



- **[Open Skills for Agentic Customer Success](https://github.com/topics/customer-success)**  

  Open-source skills library for Agentic Customer Success Operations. Hardened, multi-protocol playbooks designed for human CSMs and autonomous AI agents. Codifies technical API logic, expansion blueprints, and predictive churn-stop plays .
